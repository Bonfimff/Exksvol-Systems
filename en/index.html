Project 01

TourByFoot.com

Website developed for a tourism company specialized in walking tours and guided experiences in Brazil. The solution combines an immersive visual interface with dynamic pages to showcase tours, destinations, and experience details. The design was crafted to highlight destination imagery without compromising information clarity or the browsing experience.

Navigation & conversion

The project includes a content management structure, responsive navigation, and multiple conversion flows, letting users book directly on the page or start a conversation straight from WhatsApp.

About the development

More than an institutional website, the project was built as a web application integrated with a management system. The platform connects the visitor experience with the company's internal operations, centralizing bookings, content, clients, staff, and financial information in a single environment.

Management dashboard

Bookings can be tracked by status — confirmed, pending, or canceled — along with performance indicators such as the most-viewed and most-booked tours. The platform's own data supports decision-making and helps identify the experiences in highest demand.
